What have we learned?

H01 · ENTERPRISEPARTIALLY VALIDATED

Sustainability managers at large enterprises & PLCs struggle to turn reporting data into analytical insights that demonstrate measurable business returns.

Some enterprise interviews support the problem, while priorities differ across organizations.

H02 · SMEDEBUNKED

SME business owners struggle to turn sustainability reporting data into analytical insights that demonstrate measurable business returns.

Current interviews point instead to immediate OPEX pressures, particularly manning costs.

H03 · GREEN SMEPARTIALLY VALIDATED

SME business owners in the green space have faced restrictions from European investors and customers due to a lack of visible sustainability evidence.

Early feedback supports the direction, but more customer interviews are required.
